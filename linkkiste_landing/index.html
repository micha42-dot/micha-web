Features

Privacy & Data Sovereignty

  • Self-Hosted Approach Your data resides in your own (free) Supabase project. You have full control over the database.
  • Single-User Design Your personal, private library. Perfect for just you or a small group of friends. No social feeds, no public profiles.
  • No Tracking The app doesn't track your behavior and doesn't sell your data.

Organization & Structure

  • Tagging System Organize links with flat tags. Includes a visual "Tag Cloud" for frequently used terms.
  • Folder Structure Sort links into folders to bundle projects or topics.
  • Full-Text Search & Filters Find bookmarks instantly via title, URL, description, tags, or folder names.

Bookmark Management

  • Auto-Fill Title and description are automatically fetched from the target page when adding links.
  • Read Later & Notes Mark articles as "Unread" for later and add personal notes to any bookmark.
  • Flashback "On this day" feature shows bookmarks you saved on this date in previous years.

Design & Usability

  • Retro Interface Minimalist design, inspired by the golden era of social bookmarking (del.icio.us).
  • Responsive & PWA Works perfectly on desktop and mobile. Installable as a native-like app.
  • No Dark Patterns No popups, no ads, no distractions. Just your content.

Security & Backup

  • Full Export Your data belongs to you. Export your entire library as CSV, XML, or SQL.
  • Backup Reminder Automatically reminds you if you haven't downloaded a backup in a while.
  • Secure Authentication Login and registration are handled via secure industry standards (Supabase Auth).
